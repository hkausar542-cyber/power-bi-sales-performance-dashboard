# Sales Performance & Revenue Dashboard (Power BI)

A Power BI dashboard that analyzes sales, profit, orders and state-wise performance.

## Dashboard Preview
![Sales Performance Dashboard](Screenshot%202026-11-02%20132200.png)

## Objective
Track sales and profit over time and find which categories and states drive revenue.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- DAX measures
- Data cleaning and modelling

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales: ₹58.24M
- Total Profit: ₹27.0M
- Profit Margin: 46.4%
- Total Orders: 2K

## Key Insights
- Furniture and Electronics generate the highest profit, and Accessories the lowest
- Odisha is the top state by sales, ahead of Maharashtra, Delhi, Telangana and Karnataka
- Monthly sales move between about ₹1.6M and ₹3.2M from Jan 2024 to late 2025
- Year and Sales Channel slicers let users filter the whole dashboard
